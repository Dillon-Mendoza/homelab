# Week 9: Networking Basics & TCP/IP
**Date:** May 4, 2026
**Estimated Time:** 5-8 hours

## Objectives
- Understand your network's IP structure completely.
- Practice calculating subnets and understand CIDR notation (/24, /16, etc.).
- Configure and verify network connection parameters (Exam Objective 1.3).
- Summarize networking tools (Exam Objective 1.5).

## Key Concepts
- **IPv4 Addressing**: Public vs Private IP ranges.
- **CIDR Notation**: Subnet mask representation.
- **Interfaces**: eth0, wlan0, tailscale0.
- **Routing**: Default gateways and routing tables.
- **Common Ports**: 22 (SSH), 80 (HTTP), 443 (HTTPS), 3000 (Gitea), 514 (Syslog).
